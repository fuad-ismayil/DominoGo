# Public completed hands and move feedback

Completed rounds expose every player's remaining hand to all seated participants. Active and paused games still expose only the recipient's hand. Market contents remain private in every state. The next deal replaces completed hands with a private snapshot again.

The table renders remaining tiles beside each opponent, with their round-point contribution. The winner's summary reports the total collected from the other hands, including the lone-blank scoring rule. The local player's remaining hand occupies a separate strip above their badge when needed. Long hands are scrollable rather than cropped; all tiles stay mounted and labelled. On tall portrait phones, ordinary seven-tile hands fit in up to three rows. Short phones cap the display to preserve room for the chain.

The server derives lastPlayedTile from the newly added board tile, supporting either end and reversed orientation. Draws and passes retain it. A played tile ending the round also sets roundFinishingTile. These fields persist through reconnects and clear on the next deal. Blocked rounds have no artificial finishing tile. The client uses a restrained warm rim for the latest play and a static jade tint for a round-ending play, with no looping animation and reduced-motion support. Existing completed games without move metadata cannot reliably have their last move reconstructed; tracking applies to moves after this update.

Portrait board scale increases from a 24px to a 32px half-tile cap. Layout selection and rendering now share the same scale, while fitting still constrains long chains to the actual board bounds. Hand panels and the local remainder strip reserve their own space rather than covering played tiles.
