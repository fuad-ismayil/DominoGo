# Sound sources

Effects derive from tabletop, wood and acoustic bell foley by Kenney, released under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal.

Creator: https://kenney.nl
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Source pack: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds

Downloaded from the creator on 2026-11-02. The original license is included in this folder.
Edits: mono PCM16 WAV conversion, silence trimming, gentle boundary fades and conservative peak normalization. No synthesized tones.

The draw recordings are now brief wooden contacts (at most 160 ms), with a two-stage 1.9 kHz low-pass, 3 ms onset/35 ms release fades, peak below −11 dBFS and a further 0.65 gain in the mixer. Repeated draw feedback is limited to one sound per 180 ms. The earlier chips-handling recordings are no longer used.
